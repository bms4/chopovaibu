Chopovaibu — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

The Chopovaibu publishing tool (“the app”) is an internal tool used only by our team to publish our own videos to our own TikTok account.

What data we access

Only data of our own TikTok account, after the account owner authorizes the app: basic profile info (name, avatar, profile link), account statistics (followers, likes) and the list and status of our own videos.

How we use it

To publish our scheduled videos, to confirm which account is connected, and to show performance of our videos in our internal dashboard.

What we do not do

We do not access other users’ data, do not sell or share any data with third parties, and do not use it for advertising.

Storage

Access tokens and statistics are stored only on our own computer. The account owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok settings (Security → Manage app permissions).

Contact

Questions: send a direct message to Chopovaibu on Instagram or TikTok.
